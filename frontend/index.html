Welcome to Nyabikoni Secondary School

Nyabikoni Secondary School is a private, day and boarding school for O' and A' levels. It was founded in 2003 by Rev. Fr. Busingye Christopher, a diocesan priest in Kabale Diocese.

The school is located in Kanyakiriro Cell, Nyabikoni Ward, Central Division, Kabale Municipality; a quarter kilometre along the Kabale-Kisoro main road and approximately 1 km from the municipal centre.

The idea to start the school was born out of the need to help less fortunate children in the region access education. It is registered with the Ministry of Education and Sports under registration number Pass/N/169 and UNEB Centre No. 1998.

Our Academic Programs

Comprehensive education for every student's success

Ordinary Level (O' Level)

Our ordinary level program serves over 500 students from S.1 to S.4. Academics centers around the Uganda National curriculum with comprehensive subject coverage and excellent teaching standards.

Advanced Level (A' Level)

Our Advanced level program serves over 240 students from S.5 to S.6. We offer specialized subject combinations with modern teaching methods and excellent academic support.

Co-Curricular Activities

The school has a rich co-curricular programme that includes Music, Dance, Drama, Elocution, Games, Sports, and various Club Activities to develop well-rounded students.
